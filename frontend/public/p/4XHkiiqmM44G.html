HWFWM, Stormlight, Kingkiller, Primal Hunter, Dungeon Crawler Carl. Close behind: Cradle, Spellmonger, Lightbringer.

What lands well

Underdogs who outthink problems, warmth and humor, big payoffs. Travis Baldree, Heath Miller, Nick Podehl and Kate Reading are the narrators to look for.

What misses

Grimdark cynicism (First Law, 2★), hard SF (Dune 1★), one-and-done standalones, and long series that start to drag.
